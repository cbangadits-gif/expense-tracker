About

Expense Tracker is a small web app that helps students see where their money goes, search past expenses with regular expressions and stay under a monthly spending cap.

What you can do

  • Track expensesAdd, edit and delete records with a description, amount, currency, category and date
  • Search with RegexType a regular expression and see matches highlighted instantly.
  • Watch your budgetSet a spending cap and get a clear message when you get close or go over.
  • Keep your dataEverything is saved in your browser. Import or export it as JSON at any time.

How to use it

  1. Open Add and enter your first expense.
  2. Check the Dashboard for totals,top category and 7-day trend.
  3. Use Records to search, sort, edit or delete.
  4. Set your cap, currencies and backups in Settings

Keyboard and accessibility

  • Press Tab to move between controls; the first stop is the skip link.
  • Press Enter or Space to active buttons and menu items.
  • Messages are announced to screen readers, and colors never carry meaning alone.

Built with

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript(ES modules), no framework. Data stays on your device in localstorage.
